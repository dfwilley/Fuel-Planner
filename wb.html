Limits and sources

VerifiedEmpty weight 1,247 lb, empty CG 79.75 in, moment 99,447.30 in-lb, weighed February 4, 2024: left main 599 lb, right main 583 lb, tail 65 lb. The sheet prints the main-wheel arms rounded to 71.33 and 71.63 in; the app uses 71.325 and 71.625 in, which reproduce the sheet’s moments exactly. Tail arm 230.25 in. Arms: seats 99.83 in, baggage 129.48 in, fuel 81.97 in. Datum 72 in forward of the wing leading edge. Source: N721NE Weight and Balance (RV-14 / Registration and Airworthiness), page 1.

VerifiedCG range 82.08 to 88.24 in (18% to 29% of wing chord), gross weight 2,050 lb. Aerobatic: 1,900 lb maximum and aft CG 86.0 in (25% of chord). Source: Van's RV-14 Weight and Balance Data Quick Reference (RV-14 / RV-14 W&B Reference.pdf), Limits section.

NoteYour N721NE Weight and Balance sheet lists the aerobatic gross weight as 1,950 lb, but Van's quick reference says aerobatic flight must not be done above 1,900 lb because that is a structural limit. This app uses Van's 1,900 lb. Check which number is in your operating limitations.

VerifiedBaggage limit 100 lb (N721NE POH Rev 1.10, sections 1.10, 2.5 and 2.11). Usable fuel 50.7 gal of 50.8 gal total (POH sections 1.8 and 2.10). Fuel at 6.0 lb/gal. In aerobatic flight the baggage area must be empty (POH section 2.7), and the app flags any baggage when Aerobatic is selected.

NoteBurning fuel moves the CG aft, because the fuel arm (81.97 in) is ahead of the loaded CG. Check the landing point as well as the takeoff point. Oil is included in the empty weight (weighed with full oil). This is a planning aid; your aircraft's own weight and balance record governs.
